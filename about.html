Who We Are

The Purnea District Cycling Association (PDCA) is a registered sports body dedicated to promoting cycling as a sport and recreational activity across Purnea district, Bihar.


We organize events, training programs, and community activities to encourage fitness, sportsmanship, and healthy competition among all age groups.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission


To promote cycling as a sport and recreational activity in Purnea district, fostering community participation and athletic excellence.

Our Vision


To develop world-class cyclists from Purnea and make our district a hub for cycling sports in Bihar.
